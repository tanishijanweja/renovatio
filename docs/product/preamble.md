# Preamble

## Renovatio by Naresh Vijh

**Renovatio** is the online portfolio of **Naresh Vijh**, an architect with **20+ years of experience**. The website exists to present his body of work and practice to prospective clients and collaborators.

## Purpose

- Showcase 20+ years of architectural work across projects and typologies.
- Communicate the studio's approach, philosophy, and process.
- Serve as a credible, polished point of contact for new commissions.

## Audience

- Prospective residential/commercial clients.
- Developers, contractors, and collaborators.
- Press and design-curious visitors.

## Positioning

- Experience-led: 20+ years of practice as the core credibility signal.
- Design-forward but grounded: the site should feel like the work itself — clear, considered, and understated.
- The word *renovatio* (renewal, transformation) should be reflected in tone and storytelling.

## Design Direction

- Primary text font: **Manrope**.
- Clean, editorial typography; generous whitespace.
- Image-forward layout to lead with the work.

## Success Criteria

- A client can quickly understand who he is, what he does, and how to reach him.
- The work is the hero; the site loads fast and looks sharp on all devices.
